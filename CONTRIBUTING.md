## Legal and Community Guidelines

To keep the `DistroAV` project safe and reliable for everyone (users, contributors, maintainers) anyone involved in development or contribute to this project must adhere to the following terms.

### 1. Licensing and Patent Grant

- **GPL-2.0-or-later**: You agree to distribute your contribution of code under the GNU General Public License v2.0 or later.

### 2. Code Integrity

We always welcome every contribution! To deliver the value of your work to our users in a professional manner, please adhere to the following policies:

- **No Unverified Code**: Do not submit any code that you cannot fully understand and explain. Submitting any raw outputs from LLMs or Generative-AI is strictly prohibited.
- **Responsibility**: You must take responsibility for your code. Please ensure that your contribution has clean licensing and is reliable.
